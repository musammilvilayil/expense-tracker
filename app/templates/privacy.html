This is a student project, not a commercial service. This page describes what the software does today.

What is stored

  • Your username, an optional email address and a salted password hash. Your password is never stored in plain text.
  • The transactions, budgets, autopay rules, savings goals and reminders you create or import.
  • For each statement import: the file name, a fingerprint (hash) of the file, and counts. The uploaded file itself is not saved, and a PDF password is used once to read the file and is never stored.

How it is used

  • Only to show your own dashboard, categorize your expenses, find subscriptions, forecast spending and send the reminders you opt in to.
  • The ML runs inside the application. Your data is not sent to third-party AI services.
  • Each account sees only its own data. Administrators can manage accounts and read support messages, but the admin tools do not show anyone's transactions or other financial data.
  • Your email is used only for password reset and reminders. You can turn reminder emails off in Settings.

What is not collected

No bank credentials, card numbers or contact lists. Importing a statement or CSV is the only way bank data enters the system.

Your control

In Settings you can download all your data as a JSON file, change your password and permanently delete your account and everything stored about you. You can also delete individual transactions, undo a whole import, and delete autopay rules and goals.

Because financial data is sensitive, use a unique password, and do not enter real data on a deployment that is not served over HTTPS.
