This is a student project, not a commercial service. This page describes what the software does today.

What is stored

  • Your username and a salted password hash. Your password is never stored in plain text.
  • The transactions, budgets and autopay rules you enter or import.

How it is used

  • Only to show your own dashboard, categorize your expenses and find your subscriptions.
  • The ML runs inside the application. Your data is not sent to third-party AI services.
  • Each account sees only its own data.

What is not collected

No bank credentials, card numbers or contact lists. Importing a CSV is the only way bank data enters the system.

Your control

You can delete any transaction or autopay rule at any time. Account deletion and data export are planned but not yet available.

Because financial data is sensitive, use a unique password, and do not enter real data on a deployment that is not served over HTTPS.
