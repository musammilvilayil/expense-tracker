The AI-Powered Personal Finance and Subscription Tracker is a CSE final-year project. It combines personal finance management with machine learning so that tracking money takes less effort and says more.

The problem

  • Manual expense tracking is slow and error-prone.
  • People forget active subscriptions and renewal dates.
  • Ordinary trackers record transactions but do not analyze them.

Who it is for

  • Individuals managing their own income, expenses and subscriptions.
  • Households sharing budgets (planned).
  • Advisors given read-only access to reports (planned).

Technology

Python and Flask, SQLite, scikit-learn, and Chart.js.
