Features

Import or add

Add transactions by hand or upload a CSV (date, description, amount). Dates are normalized and duplicates removed.

AI categorization

A TF-IDF and logistic-regression model labels each expense. Fix a label once and similar ones follow.

Subscriptions

Weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly recurring charges are found automatically with monthly cost and next renewal.

Budgets

Set monthly limits per category and see warnings at 80% and 100%.

Reports and charts

Monthly and yearly summaries, category breakdown, income versus expense trend and savings rate.

Unusual spending

Charges far above your normal amount for a category are flagged, with the typical amount shown.

Recommendations

Plain-language tips on savings rate, dominant categories, rising spend and subscription cost.

Autopay rules

Recurring income and expenses are recorded on their due dates, and can be paused or deleted.
