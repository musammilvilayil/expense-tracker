How it works

  1. 1. Collect

    Create an account, then add transactions or import a CSV.

  2. 2. Clean

    Dates and descriptions are normalized and duplicates dropped.

  3. 3. Categorize

    The ML model assigns a category to every expense.

  4. 4. Detect subscriptions

    Charges that repeat at a regular interval with a steady amount are flagged.

  5. 5. Analyze

    Spending patterns, budgets and outliers are computed per category.

  6. 6. Act

    See your dashboard, alerts and recommendations, and adjust budgets or cancel subscriptions.

Raw financial data becomes actionable information. Try it.
